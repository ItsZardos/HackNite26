# Music credits

The demo includes 45 distinct recordings, released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). We selected the CC0 option where a source offered several licenses. These are excerpts of the credited works, not compositions made by Undertone or Gemini.

Clips are bundled locally in `recordings/`. We excerpted up to 60 seconds, blended the loop join, matched playback levels, and encoded stereo MP3s. Original tempo and pitch are preserved. The exact source files, hashes, edits, and durations are in [sources.json](sources.json).
